    About the experiment

    Inscriptio · Matrix errans, lex immobilis.

    FUNDIÇÃO («The Foundry» · Matrix errans, lex immobilis: «Wandering matrix, immobile law») puts Classical Purification into play: the operation through which the State extracts historical femininity from a figure and fixes it onto an eternal allegorical surface. The ten levers are the ten purification indicators of the archive itself; the die (matrix) of each batch is the real coding of that object, with the coder, date, and notes recorded in the corpus. Values are situated interpretive acts, not neutral data — and none of them is collapsed into a single score.

    Timeline

    The batches of each stage follow the year. A batch unlocks when the previous one is minted.
