NACHLEBEN

The image outlives the text. Five mottos in vector engraving on the persistence of forms, pinned as plates of a visual archive.

Tafel I

Ex imagine mens

From the image, the mind.

Tafel II

Imago superest et loquitur

The image survives and speaks.

Tafel III

Forma manens, memoria vivens

The remaining form, the living memory.

Tafel IV · Pathosformel

Pathos in figura superstes

The emotional intensity surviving within the form.

Tafel V

In umbra imago, in imagine veritas

In the shadow, the image; in the image, the truth.

Epistemic Strands

From Warburg's Nachleben to the State Allegory

In Aby Warburg's Bilderatlas Mnemosyne (1924–1929), images do not illustrate a peaceful history: they carry Pathosformeln (formulas of emotion) that survive through petrification, repression, and sudden reactivation across Atlantic time. The five plates above condense the theoretical matrix of the project:

  • Tafel I (Ex imagine mens): the gaze as an organ of thought and the generative seed of epistemic inquiry through images.
  • Tafel II (Imago superest et loquitur): the severed classical marble fragment that speaks even when legal subjects are silenced (Muta persona, loquens imago).
  • Tafel III (Forma manens, memoria vivens): colonnades and monumental endurance holding together the civic memory of the State.
  • Tafel IV (Pathos in figura superstes): Warburg's Nymph (Ninfa fiorentina), with fluid draperies and the female body summoned to personify the Republic and Law.
  • Tafel V (In umbra imago, in imagine veritas): the figure emerging from cross-hatched shadow, with historical truth revealed in time across legal forms (Veritas temporis filia).
